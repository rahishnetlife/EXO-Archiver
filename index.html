Privacy Policy for EXO Archiver

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the EXO Archiver mobile application.

Information Collection and Use

EXO Archiver does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data, device identifiers, or usage analytics. All file extraction, compression, and archive management processes are executed entirely locally on your device.

Permissions

The application requests storage access solely to read, compress, and extract files selected by you on your device. No file contents or metadata are ever uploaded to external servers.

Third-Party Services

EXO Archiver does not integrate any third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics services that collect user information.

Security

Because all operations occur locally offline on your device, your files, archives, and passwords remain strictly under your control.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via the support contact details listed on the EXO Archiver Google Play Store page.
